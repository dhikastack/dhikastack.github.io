Timeless Cuisine Resto

A system analysis and design project for Timeless Cuisine, an Indonesian restaurant serving dine-in and take-away. The work covered business process analysis, requirements, UML modelling and interface design.

  • My roleSystem Analyst & UI/UX Designer
  • TeamFour students
  • PeriodSeptember 2025
  • ToolsDraw.io · Figma

Problem

The restaurant's existing processes carried several weaknesses that the team identified during analysis:

  • Manual inventory input could cause stock discrepancies.
  • Menu availability was not updated in real time.
  • Customers had limited options for order preferences.
  • Dine-in and take-away selections were not flexible per item.
  • Procurement and inventory processes were not fully integrated.
  • Sales, stock and financial data were not centralised.
  • Management had no real-time dashboard for monitoring.

Proposed solution

An integrated restaurant information system connecting the processes that had been running separately:

  • Ordering
  • Payment
  • Inventory
  • Procurement
  • Supplier
  • Reporting

The design aimed to provide real-time data synchronisation, improve stock management, support flexible ordering, and centralise information for management.

Proposed architecture

The team proposed a three-tier client-server architecture:

Client
├── Customer tablet
├── Cashier
├── Staff
└── Manager dashboard
        │
Application server
├── Order management
├── Payment
├── Inventory
├── Procurement
├── Supplier management
└── Reporting
        │
Database
└── MySQL / PostgreSQL

What this project shows

This was the project where the analysis work carried the most weight: reading a case study, naming the real operational problems, and turning them into models another person could build from. The interface work followed the requirements rather than leading them.
