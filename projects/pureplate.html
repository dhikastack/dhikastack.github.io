UI/UX DesignTeam academic project · September 2025

PurePlate

A food delivery app concept designed to help users discover and order meals from local MSMEs without hidden fees.

  • My roleProduct Designer
  • TeamFive students
  • PeriodSeptember 2025
  • ToolsFigma

Overview

PurePlate addresses common food delivery concerns: unclear delivery costs, limited local food choices, and uncertain delivery times. The concept aims to help users discover, compare and order food from a wider range of restaurants, including local culinary businesses.

My contribution

  • Designed a food delivery app concept focused on restaurant discovery, category-based filtering and streamlined ordering.
  • Defined user flows and key feature requirements for food discovery, order placement and order tracking.
  • Developed wireframes and interactive prototypes to communicate product functionality and the user experience.
  • Conducted usability testing to evaluate the result.

Process

  • User research
  • Wireframe
  • Prototype
  • Usability testing

Concept highlights

  • Restaurant and food discovery.
  • Category-based browsing and filtering.
  • A transparent ordering experience.
  • Real-time order tracking.
  • Favourite restaurants and re-ordering.
