UI/UX DesignTeam academic project · June 2026

KFCku App Redesign

A mobile app redesign focused on improving the ordering experience, moving from user research through wireframes to a high-fidelity prototype.

  • My roleUI Designer & UX Researcher
  • TeamFive students
  • PeriodJune 2026
  • ToolsFigma

Overview

The project explored usability issues in the existing ordering experience and translated user needs into a more intuitive and streamlined interface. The redesign covered user research, wireframing and high-fidelity prototyping, with a focus on navigation, the ordering flow and the overall experience.

My contribution

  • Conducted user research to identify user needs and pain points within the existing mobile ordering experience.
  • Redesigned key user flows covering promotions, vouchers, dine-in, delivery and takeaway.
  • Developed wireframes and high-fidelity prototypes in Figma based on the research findings and requirements.

Process

  • User research
  • Wireframe
  • High-fidelity prototype

What the redesign addressed

  • User needs and pain points identified during research.
  • The ordering flow across promotions, vouchers, dine-in, delivery and takeaway.
  • Information hierarchy and navigation.
  • An interactive prototype covering the redesigned journey.
